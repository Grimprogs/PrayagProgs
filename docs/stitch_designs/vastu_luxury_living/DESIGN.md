## Brand & Style

This design system blends traditional Vedic architecture principles with contemporary high-end Indian real estate digital commerce. It addresses discerning property seekers, high-net-worth investors, and homeowners seeking spatial harmony, prosperity, and certified authenticity in their residential purchases.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic Minimalism** with **Heritage Luxe Editorial styling**:
- **Atmospheric Palette:** Warm ivories, polished walnut woods, antique gold trims, and calming Vedic foliage greens evoke sanctuary, heritage, and auspicious refinement.
- **Architectural Symmetry & Symbolism:** Clean geometric grids balanced with authentic motifs such as fine-line 16-point Vastu directional compass roses, sacred geometric mandalas, and stylized lotus seals.
- **Sensory Nuance:** Subtle parchment textures, delicate warm gold wireframe borders, and soft daylight gradients replace cold tech minimalism with welcoming tactile dignity.

## Colors

The color architecture is built strictly upon warm, grounded, and auspicious tones:

- **Primary (`#7A3E1E` - Deep Walnut Brown):** Represents stability, architectural foundation, and earthiness. Used for primary CTAs, main headlines, prominent brand icons, and high-emphasis interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#C59A48` - Antique Gold):** Represents prosperity, divine proportion, and Vedic precision. Used for decorative borders, active tab states, rating accents, compass lines, and premium badges.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D32` - Muted Natural Green):** Represents balance, vitality, and positive energy flow. Serves as the validation state for "Vastu Certified" status badges, high compatibility scores, and positive confirmation indicators.
- **Neutral Surface Hierarchy:**
  - Base Canvas (`#FDFBF7` / `#FAF6EE`): Warm natural ivory that eliminates sterile optical white glare.
  - Surface Cards (`#FFFFFF`): Crisp parchment white with subtle opacity layers.
  - Borders & Dividers (`#EADBCA`): Low-saturation sand and gold hairline strokes that maintain structure without visual harshness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of **Playfair Display** with the geometric legibility and balanced proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Headlines):** Playfair Display delivers an architectural, traditional cadence suitable for premium real estate naming, property titles, section heroes, and narrative quotes.
- **Modern Sans (Body & Controls):** Plus Jakarta Sans offers high optical clarity across micro-data sets, technical scoreboards, 16-zone breakdowns, navigation bars, and data-dense tables.
- **Numerals & Metrics:** Large metrics (such as the Vastu score `8.7/10` and pricing figures `₹ 2.40 Cr`) maintain semi-bold sans weights for effortless scanning and financial clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system utilizes a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by comfortable lateral margins that protect content breathability.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Content max-width is constrained to `1320px` to maintain comfortable eye movement.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Side-by-side verification widgets collapse into dual-stack layouts.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `0.75rem` (12px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) outer canvas margins. Complex radar diagrams and 16-zone previews reflow into vertical, expandable accordions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, multi-layered atmospheric light rather than cold grey dropshadows:

- **Border Precedence:** Depth is defined by delicate 1px warm beige-gold framing (`#EADBCA`) over pure shadow manipulation.
- **Ambient Golden Shading:** Where elevation is necessary (e.g., sticky action toolbars, floating AI assistance orbs, interactive preview modals), shadows use warm walnut-tinted blurs: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(122, 62, 30, 0.08)`.
- **Card Layers:** Elevated cards use a crisp surface (`#FFFFFF`) sitting atop an ivory substrate (`#FAF6EE`), bordered with 1px `#EADBCA` to produce tactile depth without visual clutter.

## Shapes

The geometric visual tone maintains architectural discipline through a unified `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius):

- **Cards & Content Panels:** 0.75rem to 1rem (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`) offering soft, tailored edges that echo modern craftsmanship.
- **Buttons, Selectors & Search Bars:** Rounded rect profiles with `0.5rem` (8px) corners for a balanced, grounded feel.
- **Pills & Badges:** Reserved solely for status flags ("Vastu Certified", "Ready to Move", score pills) using full pill shapes to indicate categorical, non-structural tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Walnut Fill):** Deep walnut background (`#7A3E1E`), white text, subtle gold edge highlight on hover. Icons (e.g., locks, calendars) accompany action text.
- **Secondary Action (Gold Outline):** Warm cream background, `#7A3E1E` typography, framed with a 1.5px `#C59A48` gold outline.
- **Vastu Action Variant:** Golden parchment fill (`#F5EEDB`) with `#7A3E1E` text for dedicated Vedic guidance and unlock CTAs.

### Badges & Verification Seals
- **Vastu Certified Badge:** Mint wash background (`#E8F5E9`), rich forest green border, bold green checkmark and label (`#2E7D32`).
- **Regulatory Badges (RERA, Property Type):** Warm cream surface with thin `#EADBCA` outline and muted walnut iconography.

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- **Property Hero Gallery:** Multi-image layout with rounded corners, overlay verification badges, and thumbnail pagination rings.
- **Vastu Scorecard:** Central score display (`8.7 / 10`) paired with a golden stylized lotus glyph, element-by-element directional status tags, and a summary status chip ("Functionally Balanced").
- **16-Zone Compass Radar:** Integrated 16-point circular compass visualization with muted directional axes (N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW) and layered polygon zones indicating spatial harmony.
- **Unlock Report Panel:** Gated PDF container with lock icon, layered page mockup, bulleted value proposition list, and immediate pricing callout.

### Forms & Selectors
- **Location Selector & Search Bars:** Modular segmented inputs bound by a unified warm border (`#EADBCA`), featuring integrated SVG map pins, building icons, and an embedded walnut search button.
- **Radio & Selection Modules:** Bordered card options with gold ring selectors and custom highlights for tiered purchases (Single Report vs. Multi-Pass).

### Consult & AI Floats
- **Expert Consultation Widget:** Portrait card framed in sand tones featuring certified advisors, trust indicators, and quick-contact triggers.
- **Vastu AI Assistant Button:** Circular or pill avatar positioned at the lower right, accented with gold filigree and green WhatsApp support integration.
